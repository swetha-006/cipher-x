# Research positioning

CIPHER-X is positioned as a **secure analytical and decision-support layer** for synthetic/authorized digital evidence. It does not replace CCTNS, CEIR, ICJS, forensic acquisition tools, or official case-management systems.

The research contribution is the combination of:

1. heterogeneous evidence parsing and OCR,
2. entity extraction and normalization,
3. semantic embeddings and vector retrieval,
4. cross-source knowledge-graph correlation,
5. timeline/conflict analysis,
6. evidence integrity and provenance controls,
7. access-controlled explainable RAG.

Claims should be framed as a specific system combination and evaluation contribution, not as an absolute claim that no similar research exists.
